# The surgerywaits.au name and mark

The code is free to reuse under the AGPL. The name and the identity stay with National Digital so
that people can tell the genuine service from a fork or a mirror.

**Legal basis.** surgerywaits.au does not rely on a registered trade mark. The name and identity
are protected by copyright in the original brand assets, by the tort of passing off, and by the
misleading or deceptive conduct provisions of the Australian Consumer Law (Competition and Consumer
Act 2010 (Cth), Schedule 2). Nothing here claims a registration.

## Spelling

- Write **Surgery Waits** in page text, with two capitalised words.
- Write **surgerywaits.au** in lower case for the domain and the repository.

## What is protected

- The names **Surgery Waits** and **surgerywaits.au**, and names close enough to be mistaken for
  them.
- The logo and the other brand assets, as they are added to this repository.
- The statement that the site is run by **National Digital**, and National Digital's own name and
  identity.

## Running your own copy

You may run a copy of this code under its licence. If you do, it must not look like
surgerywaits.au or say it is run by National Digital. A copy must:

- Change the site name and address, the logo and the colours.
- Replace the operator, contact, security and privacy details, which name National Digital.
- Replace every verification and registration value, such as analytics and search console tokens.
  They belong to this site.
- Keep each dataset's attribution to its publisher, and say that the publisher has not endorsed
  the copy.

## Saying you use surgerywaits.au

You may say that your work uses data or code from surgerywaits.au, and link to it. Say that
National Digital and the data publishers do not endorse your work.
