# Governance

## Who decides

surgerywaits.au is run by **National Digital**. National Digital maintains the project, reviews
contributions and is responsible for what is published.

surgerywaits.au has no government funding or endorsement. The Australian Institute of Health and
Welfare, Queensland Health and the Australian Bureau of Statistics have not endorsed it. The
maintainers make decisions in the public interest, so that people can find and compare public
hospital waiting times.

## The rules that decide the code

The project's commitments are enforced by the build. [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)
sets them out, and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes how a change is made.

## Contributing and conduct

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how changes are proposed and checked, and
[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for how we expect people to behave.
