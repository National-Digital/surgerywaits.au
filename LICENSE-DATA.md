## The data: each publisher's licence

The figures on the site come from three publishers. Each source stays under its publisher's
licence, as recorded in the [publicdata.au](https://publicdata.au) register entry for the dataset.
CC BY 4.0 above grants nothing over them.

| Source | Publisher | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MyHospitals datasets (`au-myhospitals-*`, `au-hospitals-myhospitals`) and the elective surgery waiting times datasets (`au-elective-surgery-waiting-times`, `-by-procedure`, `-by-specialty`) | Australian Institute of Health and Welfare | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), per the [AIHW copyright notice](https://www.aihw.gov.au/copyright) |
| Elective surgery by urgency category and by specialty, Queensland (`qld-elective-surgery-by-category`, `qld-elective-surgery-by-specialty`) | Queensland Health, Queensland Government | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), per the [Queensland Government open data listing](https://www.data.qld.gov.au/dataset/elective-surgery) |
| Suburb and locality boundaries, 2021 (`abs-suburbs-localities-2021`) | Australian Bureau of Statistics | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), per the [ABS digital boundary files page](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/digital-boundary-files) |

The data reaches this site through publicdata.au, which republishes each dataset as immutable
dated versions under the publisher's licence. surgerywaits.au adds no rights of its own to a
publisher's data and takes none away. Each page will name the publisher and give the
attribution its licence asks for.

The data snapshots are not in this repository. If test extracts are added, they are listed in
[`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
