Waiting times explained

Elective surgery waiting times describe how long patients wait between being placed on a public hospital list and receiving their operation. The median is the number of days within which half of the patients were treated.

Withheld figures

A hospital that treats fewer than five patients for a procedure does not publish a figure, so the page says the figure is withheld and gives the reason. Readers who need advice about their own care should speak to their general practitioner.

Source

The figures come from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare and are published once a year. Each figure carries the period it covers and the date of its release so that a reader can judge how recent it is.

Combined figures

Each hospital reports its own waiting list, and the national figure combines every public hospital that reported the procedure in the period. A state figure does the same for the hospitals in that state.
